# equivalent-expressions--formula

**Shows:** solving for a letter is the same ladder as solving an equation: undo the operations around the letter one at a time, always acting on the whole side.

**Storyboard:**
1. Formula \(P = 2L + 2W\) with \(W\) highlighted in the accent colour. Under it a short ladder: "\(W\) is multiplied by 2, then added to \(2L\)".
2. Step 1: both sides lose \(2L\): \(P - 2L = 2W\). The subtracted piece slides from the right to the left across the equals sign.
3. Step 2: both sides divided by 2. A bracket wraps the whole left side: \(\dfrac{P - 2L}{2}\); a red crossed alternative \(P - \dfrac{2L}{2}\) is shown briefly as the trap.
4. A second formula with the letter in the denominator: \(c = 3 + \dfrac{12}{m}\). The fraction is isolated, then both sides are multiplied by \(m\) and divided by \(c - 3\): \(m = \dfrac{12}{c - 3}\).
5. Final frame: the letter is alone on the left, in the accent.

**Labels & formulas:** \(P = 2L + 2W\), \(W = \dfrac{P - 2L}{2}\), \(c = 3 + \dfrac{12}{m}\), \(m = \dfrac{12}{c - 3}\), "whole side", "get the letter alone".

**Stress:** the target letter is always accent coloured; every other letter stays neutral. Each step shows the move beside the equation (for example "\(- 2L\) on both sides"). The bracket around the whole side pulses once.
