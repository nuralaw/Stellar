# nonlinear-functions--grow-decay

**Shows:** in \(a \cdot b^x\) the amount is multiplied by \(b\) every step: growth when \(b > 1\), decay when \(0 < b < 1\), both starting at \(a\).

**Storyboard:**
1. Two side-by-side bar charts, steps 0 to 4. Left: \(100(1.2)^x\), bars 100, 120, 144, 173, 207 growing taller; each step has a "\(\times 1.2\)" arrow. Right: \(100(0.8)^x\), bars 100, 80, 64, 51, 41 shrinking; arrows "\(\times 0.8\)".
2. The first bar of each chart is highlighted and labelled "start \(a = 100\)" (the \(y\)-intercept).
3. Curves replace the bars on a shared axis: the green growth curve bends up; the decay curve bends down toward the \(x\)-axis, which is drawn as a dashed line labelled "never reaches 0".
4. Percent link: "\(1.2 = 1 + 0.20\): up 20%" next to the growth chart; "\(0.8 = 1 - 0.20\): down 20%" next to the decay chart.
5. A short trap card: "0.6% = 0.006, so \(b = 1.006\)".

**Labels & formulas:** \(f(x) = a \cdot b^x\), \(b > 1\) "growth", \(0 < b < 1\) "decay", "start \(a\)", "\(\times 1.2\)", "\(\times 0.8\)", "up 20%", "down 20%".

**Stress:** growth in the accent colour and decay in a second, calm colour (not red/green only: also use up/down arrows). The first bars share one colour as "the start". Keep bar labels at least 11 px and fit all five steps on a phone width.
