# probability--independent

**Shows:** with replacement the second pick has the same fractions; without replacement the group shrinks, so the second fraction changes. Picks multiply.

**Storyboard:**
1. A bag with 3 red and 5 blue dots. Pick 1 draws a blue dot: \(\frac{5}{8}\).
2. Branch A, "with replacement": the dot returns to the bag. The bag is unchanged. Second pick: \(\frac{5}{8}\). The two fractions multiply: \(\frac{25}{64}\).
3. Branch B, "without replacement": the dot stays on the table. The bag now has 4 blue and 3 red, 7 in all. Second pick: \(\frac{4}{7}\).
4. The fractions multiply: \(\frac{5}{8} \cdot \frac{4}{7} = \frac{5}{14}\).
5. The two branches sit side by side, with the changed numbers (4 and 7) in a warm colour.

**Labels & formulas:** \(P(A \text{ and } B) = P(A) \cdot P(B)\); "with replacement", "without replacement"; \(\frac{5}{8} \cdot \frac{5}{8}\); \(\frac{5}{8} \cdot \frac{4}{7}\).

**Stress:** the numbers that change between the two branches (the 4 and the 7) in a bright colour; everything else calm. Two columns on desktop; on a phone the branches play one after the other.
