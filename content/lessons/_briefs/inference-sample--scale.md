# inference-sample--scale

**Shows:** the sample fraction is applied to the population, and a percent margin is turned into a range of counts at the end.

**Storyboard:**
1. A small row of 50 dots (the sample) with 12 highlighted: \(\frac{12}{50}\).
2. The row grows into a big block labelled "2,000 members". The same share (24%) is highlighted: \(\frac{12}{50} \cdot 2000 = 480\).
3. A bar with the estimate 40% and a margin band of 3 on each side: 37% to 43%.
4. The two ends of the band are copied onto the big block and become counts: \(0.37 \cdot 2500 = 925\) and \(0.43 \cdot 2500 = 1075\).
5. Final frame: "percent range first, then multiply by the population".

**Labels & formulas:** \(\frac{12}{50} \cdot 2000 = 480\); \(40\% \pm 3\%\); \(925\) to \(1075\); "sample", "population".

**Stress:** the population number in a strong colour so it is clear which size multiplies; the sample size in a muted colour. Big numerals.
