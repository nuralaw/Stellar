# text-structure-purpose--neighbors

**Shows:** to find the function of an underlined part, look at what it does to the sentence before it and what the sentence after it does with it.

**Storyboard:**
1. Three sentence blocks in a row: "Reefs cover less than 1% of the ocean floor." / underlined "Yet about a quarter of all sea species live on them." / "Biologists therefore call reefs the rainforests of the sea."
2. The middle block gets a bold underline and a pulse.
3. A curved arrow from the first block into the middle block, labelled "contrast".
4. A curved arrow from the middle block into the third block, labelled "reason for the nickname".
5. A verdict card at the bottom: "Function: a contrasting fact that explains the nickname".

**Labels & formulas:** `before`, `underlined`, `after`, `contrast`, `reason`. No formulas.

**Stress:** the middle block and both arrows in accent colour; the neighbours stay muted. Arrow labels must stay readable at phone width (stack the three blocks vertically on a narrow screen).
