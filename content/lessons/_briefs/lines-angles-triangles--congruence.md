# lines-angles-triangles--congruence

**Shows:** which sets of matching parts pin down a triangle (SSS, SAS, ASA, AAS) and why SSA and AAA do not.

**Storyboard:**
1. SAS. Two sides of known length leave a corner at a fixed angle (marked with an arc). A dotted line closes the triangle and snaps into one fixed place. A green check and the badge "SAS: two sides and the angle between" appear.
2. ASA / AAS. A base segment with an angle at each end: two rays from the ends meet at exactly one point. Green check, badge "ASA / AAS: two angles + a side". The third angle is shown as \(180^\circ - \) the other two.
3. SSS. Three sticks of fixed length close into one triangle (a mirror image is shown faintly and labelled "same triangle, flipped"). Badge "SSS".
4. SSA fails. A base with a fixed angle at the left end and a known side opposite it (not between). A circle arc of that radius swings and meets the base ray in two places, producing two different triangles side by side. Red cross and badge "SSA: two triangles fit".
5. AAA fails. Three equal angles with a small and a large triangle nested. Red cross and badge "AAA: same shape, different size (similar, not congruent)".

**Labels & formulas:** SSS, SAS, ASA, AAS, SSA, AAA; angle arcs and equal-side tick marks; "between" in bold on the SAS and ASA frames.

**Stress:** green (works) versus red (fails) is the main colour split; the second triangle in the SSA frame and the nested pair in the AAA frame draw the eye. Tick marks and arcs thick enough to see on a phone; each badge at 14 px or larger.
