# form-structure-sense--timeline

**Shows:** the time clue in a sentence decides the tense: a date or *since* points to a span that reaches now, *by the time* points to an earlier event on the timeline.

**Storyboard:** (canvas 320 x 180, one horizontal time arrow with "past" on the left and "now" on the right)
1. The arrow appears. A marker "2010" sits on it. The sentence "Since 2010, the museum ____ over a million visitors." fades in above.
2. A bracket grows from "2010" to "now" (accent colour). The blank fills with "has welcomed". Tag under the bracket: "up to now".
3. The first sentence fades. A second arrow row appears with two markers: "tablet in drawer" (earlier) and "team returns 1990" (later). Sentence: "By the time the team returned, the tablet ____ there."
4. A small arrow jumps from the later marker back to the earlier one. The blank fills with "had sat". Tag: "earlier event = had".
5. Hold both rows; fade out.

**Labels & formulas:** "past", "now", "since 2010", "by the time", "has welcomed", "had sat". Tags "up to now" and "earlier". No formulas.

**Stress:** the bracket / jump arrow in the primary colour; the verb that locks in in green. Wrong tries (welcomed, welcomes) shake and fade in red once. Text at least 11 units.
