# inference-sample--mean-vs-person

**Shows:** the margin-of-error range covers the population mean, while individual values scatter far outside it.

**Storyboard:**
1. A number line from 40 to 100 with a cloud of 30 grey dots (individual quiz scores). One dot at 50 is labelled "one student".
2. The mean 80 appears as a vertical line. A narrow band from 77 to 83 grows around it: "plausible mean: \(80 \pm 3\)".
3. The grey dots stay where they are. Many sit outside the band. The student at 50 is circled.
4. A speech bubble crosses out "every student is between 77 and 83" and keeps "the mean is plausibly between 77 and 83".

**Labels & formulas:** \(80 - 3 = 77\); \(80 + 3 = 83\); "mean of all", "each student".

**Stress:** the narrow band in a strong colour against the wide grey scatter; the crossed-out claim in muted red. Dots at least 10 px.
