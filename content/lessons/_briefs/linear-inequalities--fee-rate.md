# linear-inequalities--fee-rate

**Shows:** a story total is a fixed fee plus rate times quantity, compared with a limit, and whole-number answers are rounded the right way.

**Storyboard:**
1. A bar labelled "limit $50". Beside it a block "fee $15" appears at the left of the bar (it stands alone).
2. Blocks of "$6" (one per hour) stack to the right of the fee, one after another: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5. The count `\(h\)` ticks up.
3. A sixth block would cross the limit line: it flashes red and bounces away. Formula under the bar: `\(15 + 6h \le 50\)`.
4. A number line below shows `\(h \le 5.83\)` with a big dot at 5 and an arrow "round down".
5. A small note flips: for a goal ("at least") the arrow says "round up".

**Labels & formulas:** `\(15 + 6h \le 50\)`, `\(h \le 5.83\)`, tags "fee (once)", "$6 each hour", "limit".

**Stress:** fee in neutral colour, repeated blocks in primary, the rejected sixth block in red. Round-down arrow in green.
