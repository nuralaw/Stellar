# words-in-context--negation

**Shows:** a negative phrase flips the blank: first find what is true, then the blank is its opposite.

**Storyboard:**
1. The sentence "Far from being ______, the new schedule helped: students slept longer." appears with the blank pulsing.
2. A small gauge with a plus end and a minus end. A marker for the true idea ("helped") sits on the plus side.
3. The phrase "Far from" lights up and a curved arrow flips the marker across to the minus side; a second marker for the blank lands there.
4. Choice cards: "helpful" (plus side) is crossed out; "harmful" (minus side) snaps into the blank and glows green. Hold.

**Labels & formulas:** gauge ends "+" and "-"; labels "true", "blank", "far from = flip"; choices "helpful", "harmful".

**Stress:** the flip arrow is the primary motion. The gauge makes the sign obvious at 320 px; keep text to 1-3 words per label.
