# cross-text-connections--evidence-test

**Shows:** when Text 2 does not mention Text 1's claim, you test the claim against Text 2's evidence: match, partial match, clash.

**Storyboard:**
1. Left card, "Claim (Text 1)": "Old trees speed up seedling growth."
2. A prediction bubble under it: "If true, seedlings near old trees should grow faster."
3. Right card, "Evidence (Text 2)": "Same growth near and far."
4. A small chart of two equal bars ("near", "far") appears under the evidence.
5. A comparison scale between the cards swings from "match" to "clash"; the verdict "Results do not support the view" appears.

**Labels & formulas:** `Claim (Text 1)`, `If true…`, `Evidence (Text 2)`, `match`, `clash`. No formulas.

**Stress:** the prediction bubble and the two equal bars are the focus; verdict in the accent colour. Keep bar labels readable at phone width.
