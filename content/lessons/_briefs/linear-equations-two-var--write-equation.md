# linear-equations-two-var--write-equation

**Shows:** write a line in two moves: slope from two points, then plug one point into \(y = mx + b\) to find \(b\).

**Storyboard:**
1. Points \((1, 7)\) and \((4, -2)\) appear on a grid with a line through them.
2. Move 1: a slope triangle between them: run 3, rise \(-9\). Formula builds: \(m = \frac{-2 - 7}{4 - 1} = -3\).
3. Move 2: \(y = -3x + b\) appears. The point \((1, 7)\) flies into it: \(7 = -3(1) + b\).
4. \(b = 10\) is solved on the right; the line meets the \(y\)-axis at 10 (dot appears, possibly off-screen arrow).
5. Final \(y = -3x + 10\) with a check: the second point \((4, -2)\) flies in: \(-3(4) + 10 = -2\). Green tick.

**Labels & formulas:** "1. slope", "2. find \(b\)", \(m = \frac{-2 - 7}{4 - 1} = -3\), \(7 = -3(1) + b\), \(y = -3x + 10\), "check".

**Stress:** step numbers 1 and 2 in accent; the point that flies into the equation glows; green only for the final check. One formula visible per beat, readable at phone width.
