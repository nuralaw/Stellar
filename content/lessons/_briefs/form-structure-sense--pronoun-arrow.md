# form-structure-sense--pronoun-arrow

**Shows:** a pronoun points back to its antecedent and must match it in number; the nearest noun is not always the antecedent.

**Storyboard:** (320 x 180)
1. The sentence "Visitors praised the new map, saying ____ is much clearer." sits in two lines. "Visitors" and "map" are underlined in neutral.
2. The blank tries "they". An arrow from the blank jumps to "Visitors". The arrow turns red, "they" shakes and is rejected. Tag: "visitors? no".
3. The arrow slides to "map". Chip "map = 1 thing" appears (accent colour).
4. The blank locks in "it" in green. The arrow stays accent-coloured, the underline on "map" turns green.
5. A small pair row appears under the sentence: "1 thing: it, its" and "2+: they, their" in muted text. Hold, then fade out.

**Labels & formulas:** "they", "it", "map = 1 thing", "1 thing: it, its", "2+: they, their". No formulas.

**Stress:** the arrow (accent, then red for the wrong target, then green). Keep the sentence large (14-16); the labels 11-12 units.
