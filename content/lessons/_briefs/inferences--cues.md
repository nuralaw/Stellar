# inferences--cues

**Shows:** the cue word before the blank decides what kind of sentence belongs there: reason, result, contrast, example.

**Storyboard:**
1. A sentence with an empty slot at the end: "...the survey may mislead, since ______". The word "since" is highlighted.
2. A small switchboard of four labelled lamps appears: "because / since -> reason", "therefore / so -> result", "but / by contrast -> contrast", "for example -> example". The lamp for "since" lights.
3. The cue turns into a question mark bubble: "Why may the survey mislead?" and moves into the slot as a dashed outline.
4. Two choice cards: the one about cost does not answer the question (grey, "no reason"); the one about riders who chose the bus fits the outline (green).
5. The cue changes to "By contrast," and the lamp "contrast" lights; the slot outline flips its arrow direction to show an opposite.

**Labels & formulas:** the four lamp labels above, "reason", "result", "contrast", "example". No formulas.

**Stress:** the lit lamp and the cue word glowing in the sentence. Keep the four lamps in a 2x2 grid on a phone.
