# boundaries--no-marks

**Shows:** words that belong together (subject-verb, verb-object, preposition-object) take no mark between them, and a reported question ends with a period while a direct question gets a question mark.

**Storyboard:**
1. The sentence "The books on the top shelf | belong to Maya" with a long blue subject bracket and the verb in orange; a comma tries to land between them and bounces off with a red cross: "no mark between subject and verb".
2. Two more joints light up in turn: "collected | ferns" (verb-object) and "in | the forest" (preposition-object), each with a "no mark" shield.
3. A switch to end marks: a direct question "How did they escape?" with the verb before the subject (arrow swap) and a question mark.
4. An indirect question: "The team asked how they escaped" with normal order (subject before verb) and a period.
5. The two sentences sit side by side with the label "reported question = statement".

**Labels & formulas:** "subject", "verb", "object", "no mark", "direct question: ?", "indirect question: .", "verb before subject", "subject before verb". No math formulas.

**Stress:** the "no mark" shield at each joint (a gap with a red cross over any mark); in the question part the swapping arrow and the two end marks (? and .) in large type. Phone: show one joint at a time, big glyphs.
