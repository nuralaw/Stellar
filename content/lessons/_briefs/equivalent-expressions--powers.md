# equivalent-expressions--powers

**Shows:** a power is repeated multiplication, so multiplying adds exponents, dividing subtracts them, a power of a power multiplies them, and the leftover rules (zero, negative) fall out of cancelling.

**Storyboard:**
1. Row of x-blocks: \(x^3\) is three blocks, \(x^2\) is two. Put side by side they make five blocks: \(x^3 \cdot x^2 = x^{3+2} = x^5\). Block count in the accent.
2. Division: \(\dfrac{x^5}{x^2}\). Five blocks above, two below. Two pairs cancel with a stroke; three stay: \(x^{5-2} = x^3\).
3. Same fraction flipped, \(\dfrac{x^2}{x^5}\): two blocks above cancel against two below. Three stay on the bottom, so \(x^{2-5} = x^{-3} = \dfrac{1}{x^3}\) (the negative exponent is the blocks that moved down).
4. Equal powers cancel completely: \(\dfrac{x^3}{x^3} = x^{3-3} = x^0 = 1\) (all blocks gone, a 1 remains).
5. Power of a power: \((x^2)^3\) is three groups of two blocks, six in all: \(x^{2 \cdot 3} = x^6\).
6. Fractional exponent as a small card: \(x^{1/2} = \sqrt{x}\), \(16^{3/4}\): bottom 4 = 4th root (2), top 3 = power (8).

**Labels & formulas:** \(x^a \cdot x^b = x^{a+b}\), \(\dfrac{x^a}{x^b} = x^{a-b}\), \((x^a)^b = x^{ab}\), \(x^0 = 1\), \(x^{-n} = \dfrac{1}{x^n}\), \(x^{m/n}\), "same base".

**Stress:** the blocks that cancel fade with a stroke; the exponent arithmetic is shown in the accent beside them. A small red "\(x^2 + x^3 \ne x^5\)" appears at the end to mark the trap. Keep blocks big enough to count on a phone (at most 5 per row).
