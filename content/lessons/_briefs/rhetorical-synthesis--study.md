# rhetorical-synthesis--study

**Shows:** a study's notes split into aim (wanted to learn), method (did), finding (found); the goal picks the part, or two parts.

**Storyboard:**
1. Three stacked note chips drop in, left to right as a timeline: "Wanted to learn whether...", "Fitted foxes with GPS collars", "Foxes avoided busy streets".
2. Three coloured tags appear above them: "aim", "method", "finding", with a small arrow between each (aim -> method -> finding).
3. Goal "method": only the middle chip lights up and locks green; the other two dim.
4. Goal "aim + method": the first two chips light together and are joined by a bracket; a choice with only one of them shows a red "half the job". Hold.

**Labels & formulas:** "Wanted to learn whether urban foxes avoid busy streets", "Fitted foxes with GPS collars", "Foxes avoided busy streets", tags "aim", "method", "finding".

**Stress:** the three parts stay in one fixed order so students remember it. Green only on the part(s) the goal names.
