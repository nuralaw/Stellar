# command-of-evidence-textual--predict-match

**Shows:** shrink the claim to one link, predict what you would see if it is true, then match the choice that shows it; same-topic choices with no link are crossed out.

**Storyboard:**
1. The claim "a bird makes lizards lose tails" shrinks into two blocks joined by an arrow: [bird] -> [lost tails].
2. A dashed "prediction" ghost appears under the arrow: "more lost tails where the bird lives". Two small island icons: one with the bird, one without.
3. Two choice cards slide in. Card B ("lizards ate more insects") is tested against the ghost: no match, gets a grey strike line and a tag "same topic, wrong link".
4. Card A ("more broken tails on islands with the bird") snaps onto the ghost like a puzzle piece and glows green.
5. Closing caption: "Predict. Match."

**Labels & formulas:** "claim", "prediction", "same topic, wrong link", "match". No formulas.

**Stress:** the puzzle-piece snap is the focus; the ghost outline is the only dashed element. Crossed-out card turns grey and shrinks slightly. Text stays at readable size on a phone (stack the cards vertically).
