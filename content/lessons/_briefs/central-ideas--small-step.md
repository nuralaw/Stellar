# central-ideas--small-step

**Shows:** an inference question allows one small step from the text's words, not a leap.

**Storyboard:**
1. Two fact cards appear: "Boats are stuck in ice in February" and "Tomas repairs his nets".
2. A short step arrow joins them to a conclusion card: "He cannot fish, so he repairs gear". The arrow is solid green, labelled "one small step".
3. A second, long dashed arrow from the same facts leaps to "He will sell the boat". It turns red with "a leap".
4. Caption: "Point to the lines that support it. If you need a third, you stepped too far."

**Labels & formulas:** `fact`, `one small step`, `a leap`. No formulas.

**Stress:** contrast the short solid arrow with the long dashed one; green for the supported conclusion, red for the leap. Keep card text at least 12 px and stack on a phone.
