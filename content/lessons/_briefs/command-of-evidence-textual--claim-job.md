# command-of-evidence-textual--claim-job

**Shows:** every claims question has a claim (found in the passage) and a job (the verb); the same finding is right for one job and wrong for the other.

**Storyboard:**
1. A short passage as three grey bars with one bar highlighted and tagged "claim" (the sentence with *hypothesizes / concludes*). Other bars are tagged "background".
2. The question's verb appears in a pill beside it: "support". An arrow from the claim to the pill.
3. A finding card slides in ("Roofs with gardens were 4°C cooler"). It lands on a two-sided scale: the pan toward the claim lowers (green tick) because the verb is support.
4. The pill flips to "weaken". The same card now drops onto the wrong side of the scale and turns red ("wrong job"); a second card ("no cooling at all") lands on the right side with a green tick.
5. Closing frame: "Claim first. Verb second. Then the choices."

**Labels & formulas:** "claim", "background", "support", "weaken", "illustrate", "wrong job". No formulas.

**Stress:** the highlighted claim bar (accent colour) and the flipping verb pill. The scale tilt is the main motion. Keep labels at least 14px on a phone; the two cards must stay fully readable.
