# digital-sat-format--tools-by-section

**Shows:** which Bluebook tools exist in both sections, which only in Reading and Writing, and which only in Math, described by what they do (not by a fixed number of named tools).

**Storyboard:**
1. Three columns appear with headers "Both sections", "Reading and Writing only", "Math only".
2. "Both sections" fills with six icon tiles one by one: a flag (Flag a question), a struck-through option (Cross out choices), a clock with an eye (Show or hide the timer), a grid of numbered squares (Review screen), a magnifier (Zoom), a page (Test directions).
3. "Reading and Writing only" gets one tile: a highlighter over a line of text with a small note (Annotate).
4. "Math only" gets three tiles: a mini graph with a parabola (Desmos calculator), a sheet with a formula (Reference sheet), a pencil on paper (Scratch paper).
5. The timer tile shows a tiny "5:00" badge to say it returns when 5 minutes remain; the flag tile shows a small bookmark symbol (not an answer circle).

**Labels & formulas:** "Both sections", "Reading and Writing only", "Math only", "Flag", "Cross out", "Timer (hide/show)", "Review screen", "Zoom", "Directions", "Annotate", "Desmos calculator", "Reference sheet", "Scratch paper". A footnote: "Names in the app may differ a little." No formulas.

**Stress:** each new tile pops in with the accent colour and then calms. The column headers stay visible throughout so the grouping is the point. Tiles at least 40 units wide so labels stay readable on a phone. Avoid the numbers "nine" or any fixed count.
