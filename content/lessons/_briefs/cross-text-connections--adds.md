# cross-text-connections--adds

**Shows:** Text 2 can build on Text 1 as an explanation, an example or a solution, without arguing.

**Storyboard:**
1. Card "Text 1: perch in Lake Verda have declined for ten years" with a falling-arrow icon.
2. Card "Text 2: the lake's reeds were dredged; perch lay eggs in reeds" appears. A "because" arrow runs from Text 2 to Text 1, label "possible explanation".
3. The cards reset to a different pair: "Text 1: a problem" and "Text 2: a fix". The arrow label changes to "solution".
4. Reset again: "Text 1: a general rule" and "Text 2: one case". Label "example".
5. A caption: "Ask: what does Text 2 DO with Text 1's idea?"

**Labels & formulas:** `possible explanation`, `solution`, `example`. No formulas.

**Stress:** the connecting arrow and its label in accent colour; cards stay neutral. Stack the cards vertically on a phone.
