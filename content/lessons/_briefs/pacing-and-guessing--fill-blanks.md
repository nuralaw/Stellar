# pacing-and-guessing--fill-blanks

**Shows:** with the clock almost out, a blank scores zero and a guess has a chance, so every blank is filled first and improved later.

**Storyboard:**
1. A row of 6 answered question squares and 3 empty ones (labelled 24, 25, 26). A countdown "0:50" at the right. Each empty question has a small tag "needs ~80 s".
2. The countdown drops under the tag time. A caption "50 s < 80 s: no time to solve" appears and the solving idea (a pencil) is crossed out.
3. Three quick clicks: an answer circle fills on 24, 25 and 26 one after another, each with a short tick sound-less pulse and a counter "+3 chances of 1 in 4".
4. Beside the row, two small outcome cards: "blank = 0" (greyed) versus "guess = 1 in 4" (accent colour). The leftover seconds are drawn as a short bar that is then spent on question 24 only (pencil on 24, with "change the guess only if you solve it").

**Labels & formulas:** "0:50", "needs ~80 s", "blank = 0", "guess = 1 in 4", "24", "25", "26". No formulas.

**Stress:** the filled circles popping in quickly versus the empty ones; the accent colour is on the guess card. Empty circles must look clearly empty (grey outline) so the flip to filled reads on a phone.
