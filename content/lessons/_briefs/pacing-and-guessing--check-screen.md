# pacing-and-guessing--check-screen

**Shows:** the Check Your Work screen lists flagged and unanswered questions, and the order of repair is blanks first, flagged-with-answer second.

**Storyboard:**
1. A module-end screen titled "Check Your Work" with a grid of numbered squares (1 to 27). Most squares are plain (answered). Question 12 is an empty dashed square; question 26 has a small flag and a filled answer dot.
2. A timer in the corner shows "4:00" and ticks down slowly. A badge "spare time stays in this module" is shown beside it (a crossed-out arrow to a "module 2" box).
3. Step 1 highlight: question 12 pulses in the accent colour and its dashed outline fills in with an answer dot. Label "1  blanks first".
4. Step 2 highlight: question 26 pulses with a softer colour and a small magnifier; label "2  flagged with an answer". Step 3 label "3  the rest, only with a reason" fades in last.

**Labels & formulas:** "Check Your Work", "blank", "flagged", "1 blanks first", "2 flagged with an answer", "3 the rest, only with a reason", "spare time stays in this module". No formulas.

**Stress:** the order of the three labels, shown one at a time. Blank squares are visually different from answered squares (dashed outline) and from flagged ones (flag icon). Squares at least 22 units wide so numbers are readable on a phone.
