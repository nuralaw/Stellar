# central-ideas--says-does

**Shows:** in fiction, read the blurb, then compare what a character says with what the character does; the main idea keeps both.

**Storyboard:**
1. A small blurb card at the top: "Mr. Aldous has entered his roses in a village contest."
2. Two columns appear: "SAYS" with "perfectly indifferent to the prize" and "DOES" with "up before dawn; hides in the far field".
3. An arrow shows the mismatch between the columns; a "but" label appears on it.
4. A result card at the bottom: "He pretends not to care, but he does."

**Labels & formulas:** `blurb`, `says`, `does`, `main idea`. No formulas.

**Stress:** the mismatch arrow and the final card in the accent colour; the blurb stays quieter but visible. Columns stack on a phone.
