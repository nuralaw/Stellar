# Rates
Write the units. Multiply so they cancel.

## Units cancel like numbers

Write every number **with its unit**. Multiply so the unwanted unit cancels.

```example
A pump fills 15 liters in 3 minutes. At this rate, how many liters does it fill in 8 minutes?
1. 15 L / 3 min :: Write the rate as a fraction.
2. 15 L / 3 min × 8 min :: Multiply by the time. Minutes cancel.
3. 15 × 8 ÷ 3 = 40 :: Only liters are left.
4. 40 liters
```

## Set up a rate

```quiz
A printer prints 18 pages in 4 minutes. At this rate, how many pages does it print in 10 minutes?
- 180 :: 18 pages take 4 minutes, not 1. Divide by 4.
- 7.2 :: You multiplied by 4 and divided by 10. Swap them.
* 45
> 18 pages / 4 min × 10 min = 45 pages.
~ zoom -1 14 -5 60
~ type y=\frac{18}{4}x :: Pages = rate × minutes. x is minutes.
~ type x=10 :: Mark 10 minutes.
~ point (10, 45) :: Click the crossing: 45 pages.
```

# Unit conversions
A conversion is a fraction worth 1.

## Chain the fractions

Write a conversion as a fraction, like (1 ft)/(12 in). Put the unit you want gone opposite itself.

```example
A tank holds 6 gallons. How many cups is that? (1 gallon = 4 quarts, 1 quart = 4 cups)
1. 6 gal × (4 qt)/(1 gal) = 24 qt :: Gallons cancel.
2. 24 qt × (4 cups)/(1 qt) :: Quarts cancel.
3. 96 cups
```

## Convert with fractions

```quiz
A hose is 1,080 inches long. How long is it in yards? (1 foot = 12 inches, 1 yard = 3 feet)
* 30
- 90 :: That's feet. Convert feet to yards too.
- 270 :: You multiplied by 3. To cancel feet, divide by 3.
> 1,080 in × (1 ft)/(12 in) × (1 yd)/(3 ft) = 30 yd.
```

# Convert a rate
Two units, so two factors.

## Top and bottom

A speed has two units. Convert each one. 1 hour = 3,600 seconds.

```example
A car drives at 90 kilometers per hour. What is its speed in meters per second? (1 kilometer = 1,000 meters)
1. 90 km/h × (1,000 m)/(1 km) = 90,000 m/h :: Kilometers cancel.
2. 90,000 m/h × (1 h)/(3,600 s) :: Hours cancel.
3. 25 m/s
```

## Convert both units

```quiz
A drone flies at 6 meters per second. What is its speed in kilometers per hour? (1 kilometer = 1,000 meters)
* 21.6
- 0.36 :: You used 60 seconds per hour. It's 3,600.
- 21,600 :: That's meters per hour. Divide by 1,000.
> 6 m/s × (3,600 s)/(1 h) × (1 km)/(1,000 m) = 21.6 km/h.
```

# Ratios and totals
3 to 5 means parts 3k and 5k.

## Parts of k

A ratio of a to b means parts **ak** and **bk**. The total is (a + b)k.

```example
Paint mixes blue and white in a ratio of 2 to 7. A batch is 36 liters. How many liters are white?
1. 2k + 7k = 36 :: Blue is 2k, white is 7k.
2. k = 4 :: 9k = 36.
3. White = 7k = 28 liters
```

## Ratio with a total

```quiz
A jar holds only red and blue beads, in a ratio of 3 red to 5 blue. There are 64 beads in all. How many are red?
- 40 :: That's blue, 5k. Red is 3k.
* 24
- 38.4 :: Red is 3/8 of the total, not 3/5.
> 8k = 64, so k = 8. Red = 3k = 24.
~ zoom -5 70 -5 70
~ type y=\frac{5}{3}x :: x is red, y is blue: 5 blue per 3 red.
~ type x+y=64 :: Red plus blue is 64.
~ point (24, 40) :: Click the crossing: red x = 24.
```

# Trap: square units
For area, use the length factor twice.

## Square the factor

1 yd = 3 ft, but 1 yd² = 3 × 3 = **9** ft².

```example
A tile has an area of 288 square inches. What is its area in square feet? (1 foot = 12 inches)
1. 1 ft² = 12 × 12 = 144 in² :: Square the length factor.
2. 288 ÷ 144 = 2 :: Bigger unit, smaller number: divide.
3. 2 square feet
```

## Area units

```quiz
A floor has an area of 30 square yards. What is its area in square feet? (1 yard = 3 feet)
* 270
- 90 :: Trap: you used 3 once. A square yard is 9 square feet.
- 10 :: You divided. Feet are smaller, so the number grows.
> 30 yd² × 9 ft² per yd² = 270 ft².
```
