# transitions--result-time

**Shows:** a result word points forward from a cause (so), a purpose word points to a goal, a time word looks back or ahead on a line.

**Storyboard:**
1. A line with three dots: "Previously", "Now", "Later". Two chips ("Previously," left, "Later," right) hang from it with arrows looking back and ahead.
2. A cause box "Heavy rain" with an arrow to an effect box "Road closed". Chip "As a result," on the arrow.
3. A goal flag "Safer roads" with a box "New drains" pointing at it. Chip "To that end," on the arrow.
4. Hold all three rows; time row on top.

**Labels & formulas:** "Previously,", "Later,", "As a result,", "To that end,", "Heavy rain", "Road closed", "New drains", "Safer roads".

**Stress:** arrows carry the meaning (back, forward, toward a goal); primary on the arrow being explained; the rest pale.
