# probability--cells

**Shows:** on a two-way table, a row total, a column total and a single cell each give a different fraction over the same grand total.

**Storyboard:**
1. The table appears: rows Grade 9 and Grade 10, columns Car and Bus, with a Total row and column (6, 18, 24 / 14, 12, 26 / 20, 30, 50).
2. The bottom-right corner (50) pulses: "whole table".
3. The Grade 9 row lights up with its total 24. A fraction \(\frac{24}{50}\) builds: "a row".
4. The Bus column lights up instead, total 30: \(\frac{30}{50}\): "a column".
5. Only the cell where Grade 9 and Bus cross lights up (18): \(\frac{18}{50}\): "a cell = both".
6. A cell is blanked with "?" and an arrow shows "24 - 6 = 18": every row and column adds to its total.

**Labels & formulas:** \(P(\text{grade 9}) = \frac{24}{50}\); \(P(\text{bus}) = \frac{30}{50}\); \(P(\text{grade 9 and bus}) = \frac{18}{50}\); \(24 - 6 = 18\).

**Stress:** the lit row, column or cell in a strong colour, the rest dimmed; the denominator 50 stays the same colour in every frame so the eye sees it does not change. Cells at least 44 px wide on a phone.
