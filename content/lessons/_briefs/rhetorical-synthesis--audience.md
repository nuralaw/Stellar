# rhetorical-synthesis--audience

**Shows:** a new audience needs a few words saying what the topic is; an audience that already knows it does not.

**Storyboard:**
1. A speech-bubble sentence "The theremin is played without touching it." appears.
2. Left reader (question marks above their head, tag "unfamiliar"): the bubble fails to reach them; a chip "an electronic instrument" snaps in front of "theremin" and the question marks turn into ticks.
3. Right reader (tag "already knows"): the same chip tries to snap in and is greyed out with "no need"; the original short sentence reaches them.
4. Hold both readers with their matching sentences.

**Labels & formulas:** "The theremin is played without touching it.", chip "an electronic instrument", tags "unfamiliar", "already knows", "no need".

**Stress:** the added chip is the moving part (primary). Keep both readers simple, flat figures; no more than 8 words visible at once.
