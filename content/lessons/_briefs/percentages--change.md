# percentages--change

**Shows:** percent change is the change divided by the OLD value, and in "p% greater than X" the base X is the number after "than".

**Storyboard:**
1. Two bars side by side: "old 20" (short) and "new 23" (taller). The old bar is labelled "base".
2. A bracket on the top of the new bar shows the gain "+3". A dotted line copies the old bar's height across, so the gain is clearly the extra piece.
3. The gain piece is compared with the old bar: \(\frac{23 - 20}{20} = 0.15\), then the label "15% increase" appears over the extra piece, drawn as 15 small parts of 100.
4. Second scene, "200 vs 50": a short bar 50 and a bar 4 times as tall. A bracket labelled "400% OF 50" covers the whole tall bar. A different bracket labelled "300% GREATER THAN 50" covers only the extra three blocks above the first block.
5. The word "than" is highlighted in the sentence "200 is p% greater than 50" with an arrow to the base bar.

**Labels & formulas:** "old (base)", "new", \(\frac{\text{new} - \text{old}}{\text{old}}\), "15%", "400% of", "300% greater than".

**Stress:** the old bar is always the base, drawn in a steady tone; the change piece in a bright accent; the two brackets (of vs greater than) in two clearly different colours. Short labels for phones.
