# cross-text-connections--halves

**Shows:** a "difference" answer has two halves, "Text 1 …, while Text 2 …", and each half must be checked against its own text.

**Storyboard:**
1. Two short views appear side by side: "Text 1: credits the bike lanes" and "Text 2: doubts the lanes caused the drop".
2. A choice card appears below with two halves: "Text 1 credits the lanes" | "Text 2 doubts they caused the drop". Each half is joined to its text by a line; both lines turn green.
3. A second choice card with a swapped half: "Text 1 doubts the drop is real" | "Text 2 credits the lanes". Its lines turn red with crosses.
4. A third card with one good half and one bent half ("Text 2 focuses only on flaws"): the left line is green, the right red; the label "half-right".
5. Caption: "Both halves true, right owner."

**Labels & formulas:** `Text 1`, `Text 2`, `both halves true`, `swapped`, `half-right`. No formulas.

**Stress:** green and red connecting lines are the focus. Keep the two-column split on desktop; stack on a phone with the halves in order.
