# transitions--phrase

**Shows:** a phrase transition has parts that must each be true: a relation word (Based on, Undermining, Despite) and the thing it names (these models).

**Storyboard:**
1. Sentence 1 "Researchers built computer models." appears; the words "computer models" glow.
2. A phrase card slides in: "Based on | these models,". The two halves are split with a thin line.
3. Check marks: the relation half checks against sentence 2 (the estimate came from the models), the "these models" half checks against sentence 1 (they exist).
4. A rejected card "Despite | these estimates," shakes and leaves with a red cross on its relation half. Hold on the accepted card.

**Labels & formulas:** "Researchers built computer models.", cards "Based on | these models,", "Despite | these estimates,".

**Stress:** the two halves of the card are tested separately; green ticks only on the winner; red only on the rejected card.
