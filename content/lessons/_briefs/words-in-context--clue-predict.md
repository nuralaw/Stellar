# words-in-context--clue-predict

**Shows:** the blank is solved from the text: find the clue, predict your own word, then match a choice.

**Storyboard:**
1. The sentence "The new principal is ______: she answers every email within an hour and never forgets a name." appears; the blank pulses.
2. The clue "answers every email within an hour and never forgets a name" gets underlined and an arrow runs from it back to the blank.
3. A thought bubble above the blank types in the prediction "pays attention".
4. Three choice cards slide in: "distant" and "hasty" are crossed out as they fail the match; "attentive" snaps into the blank and glows green. Hold.

**Labels & formulas:** the sentence above; step tags "clue", "predict", "match"; choices "attentive", "distant", "hasty".

**Stress:** the clue underline and the arrow are the main motion (primary); the prediction bubble appears before any choice. Green only on the final word; red only on the crossed-out choices.
