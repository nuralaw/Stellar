# pacing-and-guessing--budget

**Shows:** the time budget per question is the module time divided by the number of questions, and a pace check divides the time left by the questions left.

**Storyboard:**
1. Two timer bars side by side: "R&W: 32 min, 27 questions" and "Math: 35 min, 22 questions". Each bar is cut into equal slices, one per question (27 slices and 22 slices).
2. One slice of each bar lights up and grows: the R&W slice is labelled "71 s", the Math slice "95 s". The formula \(\frac{32 \cdot 60}{27} \approx 71\) and \(\frac{35 \cdot 60}{22} \approx 95\) appears under the matching bar.
3. A cursor marks "question 8 of 22" on the Math bar. The time already used is greyed. A bracket over the remaining 15 slices reads "20 min = 1200 s left".
4. The remaining time is poured into the 15 remaining slices: each shrinks to "80 s", visibly thinner than the 95 s reference slice (drawn as a dashed outline). Caption "80 s < 95 s: a little behind".

**Labels & formulas:** "R&W", "Math", "71 s", "95 s", "budget", \(\frac{32 \cdot 60}{27} \approx 71\), \(\frac{35 \cdot 60}{22} \approx 95\), \(\frac{1200}{15} = 80\), "time left ÷ questions left".

**Stress:** the dashed 95 s outline against the thinner slices is the point (accent colour on the thin slices). Keep the two sections in different colours across all frames. Slice numbers readable on a phone (font >= 12); the bars may show only every fifth tick.
