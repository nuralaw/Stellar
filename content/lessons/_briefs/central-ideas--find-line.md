# central-ideas--find-line

**Shows:** a detail question is answered by one sentence in the text: mark the question word, find the line, match the restated choice.

**Storyboard:**
1. A short passage of three sentences appears on the left; a question card on the right: "According to the text, how did the first riders move?"
2. The word "how" and the word "first" in the question get an underline.
3. A magnifier sweeps down the passage and stops on the sentence "riders pushed off the ground with their feet"; that sentence is highlighted.
4. Four choice cards appear; the matching card has a line drawn to the highlighted sentence and turns green. A wrong card ("True, but about pedals") connects to a different sentence and turns grey with "answers another question".

**Labels & formulas:** `question word`, `the line`, `restated`, `different question`. No formulas.

**Stress:** the highlighted sentence and the connecting line are the focus; the rest of the passage stays muted. Stack passage and question vertically on a phone.
