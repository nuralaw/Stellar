# inferences--traps

**Shows:** three traps: reversed cause (the arrow points the wrong way), a link treated as a cause, and "could be true" (no sentence behind it).

**Storyboard:**
1. Two dots A and B with a solid arrow A -> B from the text (tag: "text"). A choice card shows B -> A: the arrow flips and the card is stamped "reversed cause".
2. Two clouds "dog owners" and "less stress" joined by a double-headed dotted line (tag: "text says: go together"). A choice card shows a solid arrow between them; stamp "link is not a cause". The safe card has a dotted line and the words "may be linked" (green).
3. A text bubble with three sentences and a floating thought "the bakery cut prices" that has no line to any sentence: stamp "could be true, not in the text".
4. Closing line: "Who does what to whom? Which sentence says so?"

**Labels & formulas:** "reversed cause", "link is not a cause", "could be true", "may be linked". No formulas.

**Stress:** solid arrow (cause) versus dotted line (link) must look clearly different; the flipping arrow draws the eye in frame 1. Keep stamps large on a phone.
