# command-of-evidence-textual--weaken-flip

**Shows:** to weaken, flip the prediction: the claim says "shoe -> faster", so look for the same gain without the shoe (another cause).

**Storyboard:**
1. Two lanes: "with new shoe" and "old shoes". The claim bar sits above: "new shoe -> faster". The prediction: the shoe lane drops by 8 s, the old-shoe lane stays at 0.
2. A finding card slides in ("old shoes also dropped 8 s"). The old-shoe lane bar shrinks by 8 s as well.
3. The two bars now match; a bracket between them reads "same gain" and the claim bar cracks and turns pale.
4. Four mini tags appear in a row, one per weakening pattern: "opposite result", "no difference", "another cause", "step missing". The "another cause" tag lights up.
5. A grey card "supports the claim" is shown bouncing off with a small "wrong job" label.

**Labels & formulas:** "with new shoe", "old shoes", "same gain", the four tag names above. No formulas.

**Stress:** the two bars becoming equal and the crack in the claim bar draw the eye. Keep bar lengths and labels readable on a phone; the four tags may wrap to two rows.
