# inferences--use-every-fact

**Shows:** facts chain together; the right conclusion uses every fact, and a choice that rests on a fact that is not in the text is half supported.

**Storyboard:**
1. Three fact tiles appear in a row: [drones scatter seeds] [loose soil: more sprouts] [Pell Ridge soil is loose], numbered 1, 2, 3.
2. Links draw between them (arrows 1 -> 2 -> 3) and merge into one conclusion tile: "drone seeds at Pell Ridge may sprout well".
3. A choice card "drones are the best way to plant seeds" is split into parts; one part has no fact behind it (a red gap, tag "new idea").
4. The correct choice is split into parts; each part is joined by a line to a fact tile (green), tag "every part backed".
5. Closing line: "Point to the sentence for every part."

**Labels & formulas:** "fact 1", "fact 2", "fact 3", "new idea", "every part backed". No formulas.

**Stress:** the lines from the parts of the choice back to fact tiles; the red gap on the wrong choice. Tiles stack vertically on a phone.
