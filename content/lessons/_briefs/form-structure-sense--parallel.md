# form-structure-sense--parallel

**Shows:** items joined by *and* or in a list must share one form, and a comparison must set like against like.

**Storyboard:** (320 x 180, two stacked rows)
1. Row 1: "The lock would increase the salinity and ____ a barrier." The first verb "increase" sits in an accent block; the blank is an empty dashed block of the same shape.
2. Candidates "creates" and "creating" try the slot: the shape does not match "increase" (a different-shaped block), shake, and fade in red.
3. "create" fits the shape exactly and locks in green. A dotted bracket links the two matching blocks. Tag: "same form".
4. Row 2 appears: "The population of Oslo is larger than ____ Bergen." The blank tries "Bergen" alone (a city, a different shape from "population"): shake, red. Then "that of" fits and locks in green; a bracket links "population" and "that".
5. Hold both rows; fade out.

**Labels & formulas:** "increase", "creates", "creating", "create", "same form", "population", "that of". No formulas.

**Stress:** matching block shapes in the primary colour, mismatched shapes red; the locked answer green. Keep each row on two lines at most, text 12+ units.
