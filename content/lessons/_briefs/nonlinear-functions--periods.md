# nonlinear-functions--periods

**Shows:** over several periods the multipliers multiply, so two 20% increases make 44%, not 40%, and the one-step rate is a root, not a division.

**Storyboard:**
1. A bar of height 100. Step 1: a "+20%" arrow raises it to 120. Step 2: a second "+20%" arrow raises it to 144. The bar is compared with a ghost bar at 140 labelled "20% + 20%: wrong".
2. The calculation \(1.2 \times 1.2 = 1.2^2 = 1.44\), so "+44%". The second 20% is drawn as a slice of the 120 bar (24), bigger than the first slice (20).
3. Switch to a timeline with the function \(f(x) = 50(1.5)^{x/4}\): periods of 4 units marked; two periods fit in 8 units; each period has a "\(\times 1.5\)" arrow, the total is "\(\times 2.25\)".
4. The reverse: \((1.84)^{x/4}\). One 4-unit step multiplies by 1.84; four equal small steps each multiply by \(1.84^{1/4} \approx 1.165\). A ghost row of four "+21%" steps shows \(1.21^4 \approx 2.14\), too much, with a cross.

**Labels & formulas:** \(b^n\), \(1.2^2 = 1.44\), "+44%", \(50(1.5)^{x/4}\), \(1.84^{1/4} \approx 1.165\), "multiply, don't add".
