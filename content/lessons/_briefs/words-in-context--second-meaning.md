# words-in-context--second-meaning

**Shows:** a familiar word can have a second meaning; cover it, read the sentence as a blank, and predict from context.

**Storyboard:**
1. The sentence "The mayor's plan failed to carry the council: only two of nine members voted for it." with "carry" highlighted.
2. A familiar-meaning card pops up: "lift" with a small box icon. It is grabbed by a hand, then fades (tag "meaning you know").
3. The word "carry" is covered by a blank; the clue "only two of nine voted" underlines and an arrow runs to the blank; the prediction "win over" types in.
4. Two choices, "lift" and "transport", are crossed out; "persuade" snaps into the blank in green. Hold.

**Labels & formulas:** the sentence, tags "meaning you know", "cover it", "predict", choices "lift", "transport", "persuade".

**Stress:** the cover-it step (a blank replacing the word) is the key move. Primary on the blank and clue; the familiar meaning is shown grey, then crossed.
