# statistical-claims--bias

**Shows:** a sample picked by chance mirrors the population, while a volunteer or convenience sample leans one way, and size does not repair that.

**Storyboard:**
1. A big circle of dots, the neighborhood: mixed colours (teal "likes the café", orange "does not"), about 60/40.
2. A café door at the edge of the circle. Only teal dots near the door walk in: a row of 10 dots, almost all teal. Caption: "customers who chose to come in".
3. The row's bar reads "92% yes" in the warm colour. The population bar reads "60% yes". A gap arrow between them: "biased".
4. The row grows to 40 dots, still almost all teal. The gap stays. Caption: "bigger, still biased".
5. A coin-flip icon picks dots from all over the circle: the new row matches the 60/40 mix. Caption: "picked by chance".

**Labels & formulas:** "population", "chose to come in", "biased sample", "random sample", "bigger does not fix it".

**Stress:** the mismatch between the two bars in a warm colour, and the match in a calm colour. Short captions that read on a phone.
