# command-of-evidence-quantitative--cross-out

**Shows:** two passes: first cross out every choice that misreads the table, then pick the accurate choice that does the job the sentence needs.

**Storyboard:**
1. Small table (3 museums, 2019 and 2023 visitors) on the left. On the right the sentence "Most rose, but not all: for example ______" and three choice cards.
2. Pass 1: a magnifier moves across card C ("Brook fell from 110 to 90"); the table row for Brook lights up and shows 90 -> 110. Card C gets a red strike and the tag "misread: years swapped".
3. Pass 2: the two remaining cards. Card A ("Hartley had 150") is checked against the need "a case that fell". It stays grey with the tag "true, wrong job".
4. Card B ("Quill: 80 then 65") matches; the Quill row is highlighted with a downward arrow and the card turns green.
5. Closing line: "Accurate first. Then on target."

**Labels & formulas:** "pass 1: accurate?", "pass 2: right job?", "misread", "true, wrong job". No formulas.

**Stress:** the red strike (pass 1) and the green match (pass 2) in two clearly separate steps; use two different icons for the two passes. Cards must stay readable on a phone (stack vertically).
