# boundaries--pairs

**Shows:** extra information can be removed and the sentence survives; it takes a matching pair (comma-comma, dash-dash, bracket-bracket), never a mixed pair or a lone mark.

**Storyboard:**
1. A sentence "My cousin ___ lives in Oslo" with a highlighted insert "a pilot" floating above the gap.
2. The insert drops in, wrapped by two commas that appear together: "My cousin, a pilot, lives in Oslo".
3. The insert lifts out again; the remaining line is stamped "still a full sentence" (the remove test).
4. The wrapper changes: dash pair, then bracket pair, each pair with a green lock.
5. Wrong versions flash with red crosses: comma + dash and a single comma ("My cousin, a pilot lives"), each labelled "mismatch" and "one end open".

**Labels & formulas:** "extra information", "remove test", "pair: comma ... comma", "pair: dash ... dash", "pair: ( ... )", "mismatch", "one end open". No math formulas.

**Stress:** the two ends of the wrapper are the same colour when they match (green) and different colours when they do not (red and orange); the insert is highlighted yellow. Phone: single-line sentence at the top, larger marks, caption below.
