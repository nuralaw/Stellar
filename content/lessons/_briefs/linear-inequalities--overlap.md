# linear-inequalities--overlap

**Shows:** the solutions of a system of inequalities are the points where all the shadings overlap.

**Storyboard:**
1. A grid. The first inequality `\(y > 3x - 2\)` draws a dashed line and shades above it (soft blue).
2. The second inequality `\(x + y \le 6\)` draws a solid line and shades below it (soft amber).
3. The overlap region becomes darker (primary). Label "both".
4. Points drop in: (1, 4) lands in the overlap and gets a green tick; (3, 1) lands outside the first shading, a red cross; (1, 7) lands outside the second, a red cross.

**Labels & formulas:** `\(y > 3x - 2\)`, `\(x + y \le 6\)`, points `\((1, 4)\)`, `\((3, 1)\)`, `\((1, 7)\)`, tag "both".

**Stress:** the overlap is the only strongly coloured area; the single-inequality shadings are pale. Dashed vs solid boundary must be obvious. Check coordinates: (1, 4) satisfies both; (3, 1) fails the first (1 > 7 is false); (1, 7) fails the second (8 > 6).
