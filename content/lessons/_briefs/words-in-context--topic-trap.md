# words-in-context--topic-trap

**Shows:** a choice that fits the topic is not proven by the text; only the choice the clue supports survives.

**Storyboard:**
1. The sentence "Because the old bridge was narrow and its stone was cracked, engineers judged it ______ for heavy trucks." appears; "narrow" and "cracked" are underlined.
2. Three choice cards: "historic", "unsuitable", "scenic". A proof test runs on each: a line tries to connect the card to an underlined clue.
3. "historic" and "scenic" get a dotted line that ends in nothing (a small "?" marker), then fade with the tag "true of bridges, not proved". "unsuitable" gets solid lines to both clues and a green tick.
4. Hold the green card in the blank.

**Labels & formulas:** the sentence, cards above, tags "no proof", "proved by the clue".

**Stress:** solid green line vs dotted grey line is the whole idea. Red is not used (the wrong choices are plausible, not absurd).
