# rhetorical-synthesis--emphasis

**Shows:** emphasis goals ask for the number, the sequence, or why it matters; each has a signal in the winning sentence.

**Storyboard:**
1. Three small rows, one per goal: "size", "order", "significance".
2. Size: a note "14 meters tall" slides into a sentence and the number glows. Order: two dated chips "2017" then "2018" snap onto a short arrow labelled "followed by". Significance: a chip "first Croatian woman" snaps onto a plain fact "flew solo in 1987" and a star appears.
3. Each row ends with a small checklist tick when its signal is in place; plain facts without the signal stay grey.
4. Hold all three rows.

**Labels & formulas:** "size / order / significance", "14 meters tall", "2017 -> 2018", "first Croatian woman", "flew solo in 1987".

**Stress:** each signal chip is primary when it lands; the rest are pale. Short text only.
