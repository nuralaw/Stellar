# transitions--in-fact

**Shows:** In fact pushes the same idea further (stronger detail); In other words only repeats it in new words; Moreover starts a different point.

**Storyboard:**
1. A bar for sentence 1: "Many students find the exam stressful." with an arrow pointing right along a level line.
2. "In fact," chip: a second bar starts at the same level but its arrow climbs higher (stronger). Label "stronger".
3. "In other words," chip: a second bar of the same height and length, in a different shade. Label "same idea".
4. "Moreover," chip: a second bar on a different level line, off to the side. Label "different point". Hold with the three side by side.

**Labels & formulas:** "In fact,", "In other words,", "Moreover,", tags "stronger", "same idea", "different point".

**Stress:** height = strength. In fact is the primary-coloured one; the other two pale. Short words only.
