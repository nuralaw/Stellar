# nonlinear-equations--sum-product

**Shows:** the two solutions of a quadratic are tied to its coefficients: their sum is \(-\dfrac{b}{a}\) and their product is \(\dfrac{c}{a}\), so you can answer without solving.

**Storyboard:**
1. Equation \(x^2 - 5x + 6 = 0\). Its factored form \((x - 2)(x - 3) = 0\) appears beside it, with the solutions 2 and 3.
2. Expanding \((x - 2)(x - 3)\): the middle term collects \(-2 - 3 = -5\) and the last term \(2 \cdot 3 = 6\). Arrows connect "sum 5" to the \(-5x\) and "product 6" to the \(+6\).
3. The general version: \(a(x - r)(x - s) = ax^2 - a(r + s)x + a\,rs\), giving \(r + s = -\dfrac{b}{a}\) and \(rs = \dfrac{c}{a}\).
4. Use it: \(5x^2 - 30x + 8 = 0\): \(a = 5\), \(b = -30\), \(c = 8\), sum \(6\), product \(\dfrac85\) appear without solving.
5. The trap frame: \(x^2 - 6x + 9 = 0\) has one solution (3), so "two different solutions only".

**Labels & formulas:** \((x - 2)(x - 3)\), \(r + s = -\dfrac{b}{a}\), \(rs = \dfrac{c}{a}\), "two different solutions".

**Stress:** the sum pieces share one colour and the product pieces another, from the expansion to the formula. The trap frame is short. Keep formulas at least 14 px.
