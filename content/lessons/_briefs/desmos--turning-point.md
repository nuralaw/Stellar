# desmos--turning-point

**Shows:** the top or bottom of a curve has two coordinates, and the maximum or minimum value is the y-coordinate while the x-coordinate says when or where it happens.

**Storyboard:**
1. A downward parabola \(h(t) = -5t^2 + 30t + 4\) is drawn (axes labelled t and h in the word-problem version, x and y in the retyped version). A small chip shows the retyping "t becomes x".
2. A gray dot lights up on the vertex and a tooltip pops out: \((3, 49)\).
3. Two arrows leave the dot: one down to the x-axis labelled "x = 3: when (seconds)", one left to the y-axis labelled "y = 49: maximum value (meters)".
4. A question strip swaps between three prompts, each pointing at one arrow: "maximum height?" lights the y arrow, "at what time?" lights the x arrow, "zeros?" lights the two x-intercepts.

**Labels & formulas:** \(h(t) = -5t^2 + 30t + 4\), \((3, 49)\), "when", "maximum value", "zeros".

**Stress:** the two arrows in two clearly different colours, and the matching prompt highlighting its arrow. Keep the vertex dot large (at least 10 units). The words "max value = y" must stay on screen in the last frame.
