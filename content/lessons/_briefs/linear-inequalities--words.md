# linear-inequalities--words

**Shows:** each word cue ("more than", "at least", "at most", "between") becomes a symbol and a shape on a number line.

**Storyboard:**
1. A number line from 0 to 10 with ticks.
2. "more than 4" slides in; an open circle at 4 with an arrow right appears; symbol `\(x > 4\)`.
3. "at least 4" replaces it; the circle fills in (closed); `\(x \ge 4\)`.
4. "at most 7": closed circle at 7, arrow left; `\(x \le 7\)`.
5. "between 4 and 7": the two ends joined by a thick bar; `\(4 < x < 7\)`; then the ends fill in for "inclusive": `\(4 \le x \le 7\)`.

**Labels & formulas:** words "more than", "at least", "at most", "between"; formulas above.

**Stress:** open vs filled circle is the key contrast (primary outline vs primary fill). The words and the symbol always sit above the line, together. Keep words to 1-3 per frame.
