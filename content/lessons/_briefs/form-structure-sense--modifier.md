# form-structure-sense--modifier

**Shows:** the opening phrase before the comma describes the subject right after the comma; a wrong noun there makes the modifier dangle.

**Storyboard:** (320 x 180)
1. Blocks side by side: a phrase block "Founded by a retired nurse," (accent outline), a comma, and an empty slot block.
2. Candidate "3,000 patients" slides into the slot. A line from the phrase to it shows "founded?" and is crossed out in red; the block shakes and leaves.
3. Candidate "a new wing" tries the same: crossed out in red.
4. Candidate "the clinic" slides in. The line from the phrase to it turns green with a check; the slot fills green.
5. The rest of the sentence ("treats patients every year.") fades in after the slot. Hold, then fade out.

**Labels & formulas:** "Founded by a retired nurse,", "3,000 patients", "a new wing", "the clinic", "who or what was founded?". No formulas.

**Stress:** the connecting line between phrase and subject; red for the two rejected nouns, green for the right one. Phrase and slot must stay readable on a phone (text 13+ units).
