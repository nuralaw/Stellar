# boundaries--however-slot

**Shows:** however/therefore are not FANBOYS: between two sentences the word takes a semicolon (or period) before and a comma after, and it can slide to other spots.

**Storyboard:**
1. Two full-sentence blocks side by side: "The museum was free" and "the line was long". The gap is empty.
2. A FANBOYS chip "but" enters with a comma and locks green. Then the chip "however" tries the same way (", however") and is rejected with a red shake and the label "comma splice".
3. The correct setup: a semicolon drops before "however" and a comma after it; the whole line turns green: "; however,".
4. The word "however" slides inside the second sentence ("The line, however, was long") and a pair of commas wraps it: "inside one sentence: two commas".
5. The word slides to the end of the first block: ", however;" with the caption "ends the sentence it contrasts with".

**Labels & formulas:** "FANBOYS: , but", "not FANBOYS: however, therefore, moreover", "; however,", ". However,", "pair of commas". No math formulas.

**Stress:** the slot for punctuation marks is a thick pulsing frame; the word "however" is a moving chip in a distinct colour (purple). Red shake for the rejection, green lock for success. Phone: keep the two sentence blocks stacked vertically with large punctuation glyphs.
