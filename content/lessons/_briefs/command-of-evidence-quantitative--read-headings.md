# command-of-evidence-quantitative--read-headings

**Shows:** read the title, row and column headings and units before any number; a table cell is found where a row and a column meet, and units can swap the meaning of a number.

**Storyboard:**
1. A small table appears greyed out (3 rows x 3 columns: Tank, Water temp (°C), Fish length (cm)) with the title above it. The title and the two column headings pulse in turn with the tags "title", "column heading", "unit".
2. The question phrase "the warmer tank" appears. A horizontal band sweeps along the rows and stops at Tank B (24); a vertical band sweeps along the columns and stops at the right column. The cell where they cross glows.
3. A wrong choice card "fish 24 cm long" tries to use the 24 from the temperature column; a red arrow shows the number being moved to the wrong column, and the card is stamped "wrong column".
4. A second mini-table with a "years before present" column: a small arrow reverses so the largest number is marked "oldest".
5. Closing line: "Labels first. Then numbers."

**Labels & formulas:** "title", "row", "column", "unit", "wrong column", "years before present: bigger = older". No formulas.

**Stress:** the crossing bands and the glowing cell are the main motion. Keep the table at least 14px text on a phone; keep only 3 columns visible.
