# transitions--add-example

**Shows:** three ways a second sentence can add to the first: a separate new point (Moreover), one case of the idea (For example), the exact detail of the idea (Specifically).

**Storyboard:**
1. A first sentence block: "The library offers free help." (a wide pale bar).
2. Three small second-sentence blocks slide in one by one under it, each with its connector chip above: "Moreover," beside a block placed NEXT to the first block (a new, equal bar); "For example," beside a small block that sits INSIDE the first bar (one case); "Specifically," beside a block that zooms in on one spot of the first bar (magnifier ring, a detail).
3. Each pair shows its relation by position: side by side, inside, zoomed.
4. Hold. The three chips stay; the first sentence stays.

**Labels & formulas:** "The library offers free help." Chips "Moreover,", "For example,", "Specifically,". Tags under the blocks: "new point", "one case", "exact detail".

**Stress:** position tells the story (next to / inside / zoomed). Primary colour on the chip and block being explained; the others pale. Text readable at 320 px wide; no more than 8 words visible at once.
