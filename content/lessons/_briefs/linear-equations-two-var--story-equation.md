# linear-equations-two-var--story-equation

**Shows:** in \(8x + 5y = 200\) each coefficient is the price of one item, each term is money spent on that item, and the intercepts mean "only one kind".

**Storyboard:**
1. Two item icons: a T-shirt "$8" and a hat "$5", with counters \(x\) and \(y\).
2. Equation builds: \(8x\) (shirt money) + \(5y\) (hat money) = 200 (total). Each term lights up with its icon.
3. Plug in \(x = 15\): shirt pile of 15 icons turns into \(8(15) = 120\); the rest of the bar "80 left" is filled with hats: \(5y = 80\), \(y = 16\).
4. The line on a small grid: the \(x\)-intercept \((25, 0)\) labelled "only shirts", the \(y\)-intercept \((0, 40)\) labelled "only hats", and a point \((15, 16)\) marked on the line.

**Labels & formulas:** "price × count", \(8x + 5y = 200\), \(8(15) + 5y = 200\), \(y = 16\), "only shirts", "only hats".

**Stress:** the coefficient numbers 8 and 5 are the accent; the counters \(x\), \(y\) neutral. Intercept labels short (≤ 2 words). Keep icon pile ≤ 8 icons, then "×15" badge.
