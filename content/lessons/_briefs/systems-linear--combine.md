# systems-linear--combine

**Shows:** when the question asks for `x + y` (or `3x + 3y`), adding or subtracting the whole equations answers it at once, without solving for x and y separately.

**Storyboard:**
1. Two equations stacked: `\(4x + 5y = 100\)` and `\(5x + 4y = 62\)`. Below them a question tag: `\(x + y = \;?\)`.
2. A "+" sign slides between the two equations. The left sides slide together and merge into `\(9x + 9y\)`; the right sides merge into `\(162\)`.
3. A division bar appears: `\(\div 9\)` on both sides. Result: `\(x + y = 18\)` highlighted.
4. A second small row (greyed): "subtract instead" shows `\(-x + y = 38\)`, to show subtraction is an option when it gives what is asked.

**Labels & formulas:** `\(4x + 5y = 100\)`, `\(5x + 4y = 62\)`, `\(9x + 9y = 162\)`, `\(x + y = 18\)`, caption "Read the question first".

**Stress:** the question tag `\(x + y\)` is primary colour at the start and stays visible; the matching `9x + 9y` terms glow in primary; the final answer turns green. Do not show the x and y values at all (that is the point).
