# text-structure-purpose--strength

**Shows:** a verb must match the strength of the text: a text that says "usually" qualifies a claim, it does not refute it.

**Storyboard:**
1. A horizontal strength bar from "mild" (left) to "strong" (right) with verb markers: supports, concedes, qualifies, challenges, refutes.
2. A text strip above it: "They usually do ... But in very short falls, they lack the time to turn." The words "usually" and "But in very short falls" are highlighted.
3. A pointer drops from the highlighted words onto "qualifies" on the bar; the marker glows.
4. A second pointer tries "refutes", flashes red with a cross and the note "too strong".
5. Final caption: "Match the verb to how far the text goes".

**Labels & formulas:** the six verbs, `mild`, `strong`, `too strong`. No formulas.

**Stress:** the matched verb glows in the accent colour; the rejected verb turns the "wrong" colour for a moment. Verb labels must not overlap on a phone (rotate or abbreviate the bar into two rows if needed).
