# digital-sat-format--order

**Shows:** in Math a module climbs steadily from easy to hard; in Reading and Writing a module runs through four fixed groups whose difficulty only roughly rises.

**Storyboard:**
1. Top lane "Math, 22 questions": 22 thin bars grow left to right, each taller than the one before. A caption "easiest" under the first bar and "hardest" under the last. The last few bars get the accent colour.
2. Bottom lane "Reading and Writing, 27 questions": 27 bars appear in four blocks separated by small gaps, each block labelled below: "Craft and Structure", "Information and Ideas", "Conventions", "Expression of Ideas".
3. The R&W bars grow in the order of the blocks, but the heights are only roughly rising: a few bars dip below their left neighbour (a hard bar followed by an easy one). A dashed rising trend line is drawn through them and labelled "roughly".
4. Inside the Conventions block the bars are drawn in mixed colours (skills mixed); inside the other three blocks the bars share one colour per skill, as small runs. A small legend: "same skill together" (one colour) and "mixed skills" (Conventions).
5. Final frame: "Math: strictly easy to hard" over the top lane, "R&W: only roughly" over the bottom lane.

**Labels & formulas:** "Math", "R&W", "easiest", "hardest", "roughly", "Craft and Structure", "Information and Ideas", "Conventions", "Expression of Ideas", "mixed skills". No formulas.

**Stress:** the contrast between the smooth Math staircase and the jagged R&W trend (draw the dips in the accent colour). The word "roughly" must be readable on a phone (font ≥ 12). Group names may be shortened to "Craft", "Ideas", "Conventions", "Expression" if space is tight, but the full names go in the alt text.
