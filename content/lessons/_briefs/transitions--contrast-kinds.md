# transitions--contrast-kinds

**Shows:** four kinds of "but": Still (turns against the expectation), By contrast (two things side by side), Instead (the real thing replaces the expected one), On the contrary (rejects the claim).

**Storyboard:**
1. Four small panels in a 2x2 grid, each with a one-word chip.
2. "Still,": an arrow goes up toward "bad reviews", then a second arrow shoots past it: "big sales".
3. "By contrast,": two columns with a bar each, one tall, one short.
4. "Instead,": an expected box with a dashed outline is crossed out and a solid box slides in to replace it.
5. "On the contrary,": a statement bubble gets a red cross and a bubble with the opposite appears. Hold.

**Labels & formulas:** chips "Still,", "By contrast,", "Instead,", "On the contrary,"; tags "despite", "side by side", "replaces", "rejects".

**Stress:** one panel at a time in primary, others pale. Each panel must read at 320 px with at most 3 words inside.
