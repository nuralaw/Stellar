# text-structure-purpose--traps

**Shows:** a wrong answer can be true and still miss the job: too narrow, too broad, recycled words, or an invented second half.

**Storyboard:**
1. A frame the size of a whole text (dashed outline) with three sentence bars inside it.
2. Choice card "Saffron is a spice" lands on only the first bar: it shrinks and a label "too narrow" appears.
3. Choice card "Spices in the world" overflows the frame on all sides: label "too broad".
4. Choice card "Why saffron is costly: the hand harvest" fits the frame and covers all three bars; it turns green with "covers the whole text".
5. Last, a card with two halves where the second half is hatched grey: "then explains why it is empty" with the note "invented half".

**Labels & formulas:** `too narrow`, `too broad`, `fits the whole text`, `invented half`. No formulas.

**Stress:** green for the fitting card, red edge for the others; the dashed frame stays visible throughout. Choice text at least 12 px; cards stack vertically on a phone.
