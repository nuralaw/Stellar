# text-structure-purpose--turn

**Shows:** the author's own point comes after the turn word, and the purpose names the job of the whole text.

**Storyboard:**
1. Two sentences appear as blocks: "Most people think the tongue has separate zones for taste." and "Actually, researchers have shown that every part can sense every taste."
2. A small job label slides above sentence 1: "common belief" (muted colour).
3. The word "Actually" lights up and a swing arrow (left to right) appears between the blocks: "turn".
4. A label appears above sentence 2: "evidence against it" (accent colour).
5. A purpose card drops in below both: "To correct a common belief about taste". Both blocks send a thin line into it.
6. A small note fades in under the turn: "In fact = emphasis, not a turn".

**Labels & formulas:** labels `common belief`, `turn`, `evidence against it`, `Purpose: To correct a common belief`. No formulas.

**Stress:** accent colour and a pulse on the turn word and on the final purpose card; sentence 1 stays muted. Labels at least 12 px on a phone; the two sentences wrap into two lines each, never smaller.
