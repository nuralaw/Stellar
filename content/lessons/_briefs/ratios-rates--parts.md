# ratios-rates--parts

**Shows:** a ratio of 2 to 7 is 2 parts and 7 parts of the same size \(k\); the total is 9 parts, so one part is the total divided by 9.

**Storyboard:**
1. A long bar of 9 equal blocks appears empty, with the bracket "36 liters" over the whole bar.
2. The first 2 blocks fill blue and the next 7 fill white. Labels under them: "blue \(2k\)" and "white \(7k\)". The ratio "2 to 7" sits above.
3. A bracket joins all 9 blocks: \(2k + 7k = 9k = 36\). The equation is written beside the bar.
4. The 36 liters split evenly into the 9 blocks: each block shows "4 L" and the symbol \(k = 4\) is highlighted on one block.
5. The 7 white blocks glow and a bracket shows \(7k = 28\) L as the answer. A faded side note shows "white is \(\frac{7}{9}\) of the total, not \(\frac{2}{7}\)".

**Labels & formulas:** "blue \(2k\)", "white \(7k\)", \(9k = 36\), \(k = 4\), "28 L".

**Stress:** the equal block size (one block = one part = \(k\)); blue and white in two clearly different tones; the white blocks pulse at the end. Keep labels readable on a phone: at most the numbers 2, 7, 9, 4, 28 plus the letter \(k\).
