# rhetorical-synthesis--goal-first

**Shows:** read the goal first; each true choice is tested against the job, and only the one that does the job locks in.

**Storyboard:**
1. Three note chips appear on the left: "Lund was a sculptor", "Tide Gate is in Bergen harbor", "Tide Gate is 14 meters tall".
2. A goal tag drops in on top: "Goal: emphasize height" with the word "height" in primary.
3. Three choice sentences, one per note, slide in on the right. Each is tested against the goal: the first two get a grey "true, wrong job" mark and dim; the third ("Tide Gate is 14 meters tall.") gets a green tick and a line back to its note.
4. Hold: the green sentence and its note are linked; the goal tag stays visible.

**Labels & formulas:** notes as above, goal "emphasize height", choices "Lund made Tide Gate.", "Tide Gate stands in Bergen.", "Tide Gate is 14 meters tall.", tags "true, wrong job", "does the job".

**Stress:** the goal word is the only primary-coloured text until the winner locks in green. Wrong choices are dimmed, not red (they are true). Short text, at most 8 words visible at a time besides the three sentences.
