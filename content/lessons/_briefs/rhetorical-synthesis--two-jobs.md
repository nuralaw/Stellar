# rhetorical-synthesis--two-jobs

**Shows:** a goal with "and" asks for two jobs; a sentence must tick both boxes.

**Storyboard:**
1. A goal card appears with two empty tick boxes: "define keystone species" and "give an example".
2. Three choice sentences slide in one at a time. Sentence A ("A keystone species shapes its ecosystem.") ticks only the first box and is marked "half". Sentence B ("Sea otters protect kelp forests.") ticks only the second box, "half".
3. Sentence C ("A keystone species, such as the sea otter, shapes its ecosystem.") ticks both boxes and locks green.
4. Hold the green sentence.

**Labels & formulas:** goal boxes "define", "example"; the three sentences above; tags "half", "both".

**Stress:** the two boxes are the focus. Red-ish "half" marks only on A and B; green only on C.
