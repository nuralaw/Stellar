# probability--fraction

**Shows:** probability is the outcomes that fit divided by all the outcomes, it lives between 0 and 1, and "not" is the rest.

**Storyboard:**
1. A jar of 20 dots in three colours: 5 red, 7 green, 8 blue. The count "20 marbles" appears under it.
2. The 7 green dots glow. A label reads "fit: 7". All 20 dots get a soft outline: "all: 20". The fraction \(\frac{7}{20}\) builds up between them.
3. A number line from 0 to 1 slides in. A marker lands on 0.35. Ends are labelled "impossible" (0) and "certain" (1).
4. The 13 non-green dots glow instead. The line shows \(1 - \frac{7}{20} = \frac{13}{20}\) with the marker jumping to 0.65.
5. Final frame: the formula and the two fractions side by side.

**Labels & formulas:** \(P = \frac{\text{outcomes that fit}}{\text{all possible outcomes}}\); \(P(\text{green}) = \frac{7}{20}\); \(P(\text{not green}) = 1 - \frac{7}{20} = \frac{13}{20}\); "impossible", "certain".

**Stress:** the "fit" dots in a strong colour on a muted rest; the denominator always outlined around the whole jar. Fractions stay large and readable on a phone; do not show percents.
