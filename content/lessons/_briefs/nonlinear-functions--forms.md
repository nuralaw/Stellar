# nonlinear-functions--forms

**Shows:** the same parabola written as factored, vertex and standard form; each form makes one feature readable at a glance (zeros, vertex, \(y\)-intercept).

**Storyboard:**
1. The parabola \(y = x^2 - 10x + 16\) drawn once, with the three features marked as hollow dots: zeros at 2 and 8, vertex at \((5, -9)\), \(y\)-intercept at \((0, 16)\).
2. The factored form \((x - 2)(x - 8)\) fades in; the two zero dots fill in with the numbers 2 and 8 flying out of the brackets (each bracket set to 0).
3. The vertex form \((x - 5)^2 - 9\) replaces it; the vertex dot fills in with 5 and \(-9\) flying out.
4. The standard form \(x^2 - 10x + 16\) replaces that; the \(y\)-intercept dot fills with 16.
5. A strip shows all three equal signs side by side with a caption "same graph, three views".
6. A short marker shows the vertex halfway between the zeros: \(\dfrac{2 + 8}{2} = 5\).

**Labels & formulas:** \((x - 2)(x - 8)\), \((x - 5)^2 - 9\), \(x^2 - 10x + 16\), "zeros", "vertex", "\(y\)-intercept", \(\dfrac{2 + 8}{2} = 5\).

**Stress:** each form has its own colour, and the feature it shows has the same colour on the graph. Only one feature is lit at a time. Keep the graph in the upper half and the form text at least 14 px for phones.
