# percentages--go-back

**Shows:** to get back to the original, divide the result by the multiplier; taking the percent of the result gives the wrong answer.

**Storyboard:**
1. A bar labelled "?" (the old price) sits at 100%. A downward arrow labelled "-10%" shrinks it to a 90% bar with the label "$72".
2. The wrong move appears and is struck out: "10% of 72 = 7.2, so 79.2" with a red cross. Caption "The 10% was taken from the old price".
3. The 90% bar is split into 9 equal blocks of $8 each (72 divided by 9).
4. A tenth block is added, restoring the full bar: 10 blocks of $8. The equation \(\text{old} = \frac{72}{0.9} = 80\) appears.
5. A small closing line: forward check \(80 \times 0.9 = 72\), with the 80 bar and 72 bar overlapping.

**Labels & formulas:** "old = ?", "now $72", \(\frac{72}{0.9} = 80\), \(80 \times 0.9 = 72\), "10% of 72" (struck out).

**Stress:** the splitting of the 90% bar into equal blocks and adding the missing block is the key motion; the wrong move is red and quick; the final $80 in green. Readable at phone size with 4 labels at most at a time.
