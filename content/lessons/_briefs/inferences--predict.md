# inferences--predict

**Shows:** read the text as roles (belief, twist, cue), write the conclusion yourself before the choices, then match it.

**Storyboard:**
1. Three text bars stack up: "Many sailors believed fog slows ships" (tag: belief), "But ships moved just as fast in fog" (tag: twist, accent colour), "This suggests that ______" (tag: cue, with an empty slot).
2. An arrow runs from the belief and the twist into the empty slot; a thought bubble appears beside it: "So fog is not the cause."
3. The bubble text settles into the slot as a dashed "prediction" chip.
4. Two choice cards slide in. The card that only repeats a fact ("ships moved as fast in fog") bounces off, tagged "repeats a fact". The card that matches the prediction snaps into the slot and turns green.
5. Closing line: "Predict first. Then match."

**Labels & formulas:** "belief", "twist", "cue", "prediction", "repeats a fact", "match". No formulas.

**Stress:** the twist bar (accent colour) and the prediction chip snapping into the slot. Show the prediction BEFORE the choices appear. Text stays readable on a phone (stack bars vertically).
