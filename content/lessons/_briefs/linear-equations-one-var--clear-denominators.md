# linear-equations-one-var--clear-denominators

**Shows:** multiplying EVERY term (both sides) by the same number turns a fraction or decimal equation into a whole-number one.

**Storyboard:**
1. The equation \(\frac{x}{2} + \frac{x}{3} = 10\) appears, the denominators 2 and 3 are circled.
2. A badge "× 6" drops in front of the whole equation; arrows from it touch each of the three terms (both fractions and the 10) one by one.
3. Each term transforms: \(\frac{x}{2} \to 3x\), \(\frac{x}{3} \to 2x\), \(10 \to 60\). The denominators slide away.
4. Result \(3x + 2x = 60\), then \(x = 12\). Hold.
5. (Small second row, optional) \(0.4x + 1.5 = 0.25x + 3\) with a "× 100" badge turning into \(40x + 150 = 25x + 300\).

**Labels & formulas:** "× 6" (large badge), "every term", \(\frac{x}{2} + \frac{x}{3} = 10\), \(3x + 2x = 60\), \(x = 12\).

**Stress:** the plain number 10 getting multiplied too (the common miss): give it the accent colour when "× 6" hits it. Denominators fade out in neutral. Readable at phone width: one equation per line, ≥ 14 units tall.
