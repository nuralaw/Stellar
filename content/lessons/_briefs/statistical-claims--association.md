# statistical-claims--association

**Shows:** a survey sees two things moving together (an association) but cannot say that one makes the other change.

**Storyboard:**
1. A scatter plot: hours of exercise on the x-axis, quality of sleep on the y-axis. Dots rise to the right. A trend line appears.
2. The label "association: they go together" lands on the line.
3. An arrow "exercise -> sleep" appears with a question mark. A second arrow "sleep -> exercise" fades in with a question mark.
4. A third box, "stress" (or "age"), pops above with arrows to both axes: another possible explanation.
5. Final frame: the word "cause" crossed out and the words "is associated with" ticked.

**Labels & formulas:** "exercise", "sleep", "association", "cause?", "a third factor".

**Stress:** the trend line and the "association" label in the strong colour; the question-mark arrows in a muted colour. Keep text short for 320 px.
