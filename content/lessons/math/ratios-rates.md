## Units cancel like numbers

Write every number **with its unit**. Multiply so the unit you don't want cancels.

```example
A pump fills 15 liters in 3 minutes. At this rate, how many liters does it fill in 8 minutes?
1. 15 L / 3 min :: Write the rate as a fraction.
2. 15 L / 3 min × 8 min :: Multiply by the time. Minutes cancel.
3. 15 × 8 ÷ 3 = 40 :: Only liters are left.
4. 40 liters
```

## Set up a rate

```quiz
A printer prints 18 pages in 4 minutes. At this rate, how many pages does it print in 10 minutes?
* 45
- 180 :: 18 pages take 4 minutes, not 1. Divide by 4.
- 7.2 :: You multiplied by 4 and divided by 10. Swap them.
> 18 pages / 4 min × 10 min = 45 pages.
```

## Convert with fractions

A conversion is a fraction worth 1, like (1 ft)/(12 in). Put the unit you want gone on the opposite level.

```quiz
A hose is 1,080 inches long. How long is it in yards? (1 foot = 12 inches, 1 yard = 3 feet)
* 30
- 90 :: That's feet. Convert feet to yards too.
- 270 :: You multiplied by 3. To cancel feet, divide by 3.
> 1,080 in × (1 ft)/(12 in) × (1 yd)/(3 ft) = 30 yd.
```

## Rates: convert both units

In meters per second, the top and the bottom each need a factor. 1 hour = 3,600 seconds.

```quiz
A drone flies at 6 meters per second. What is its speed in kilometers per hour? (1 kilometer = 1,000 meters)
* 21.6
- 0.36 :: You used 60 seconds per hour. It's 3,600.
- 21,600 :: That's meters per hour. Divide by 1,000.
> 6 m/s × (3,600 s)/(1 h) × (1 km)/(1,000 m) = 21.6 km/h.
```

## Ratio with a total

A ratio of 3 to 5 means parts **3k** and **5k**. The total is 8k.

```quiz
A jar holds only red and blue beads, in a ratio of 3 red to 5 blue. There are 64 beads in all. How many are red?
* 24
- 40 :: That's blue, 5k. Red is 3k.
- 38.4 :: Red is 3/8 of the total, not 3/5.
> 8k = 64, so k = 8. Red = 3k = 24.
```

## Trap: square units

1 yd = 3 ft, but 1 yd² = 3 × 3 = **9** ft². For area, use the factor twice.

```quiz
A floor has an area of 30 square yards. What is its area in square feet? (1 yard = 3 feet)
* 270
- 90 :: Trap: you used 3 once. A square yard is 9 square feet.
- 10 :: You divided. Feet are smaller, so the number grows.
> 30 yd² × 9 ft² per yd² = 270 ft².
```

## Recap

- Write units on every number. Cancel them like numbers.
- Ratio a to b: parts ak and bk, total (a + b)k.
- Area units: use the conversion factor twice.
