# central-ideas--strength

**Shows:** the strength of a choice's words must match the strength of the text: some is not all, may is not proves.

**Storyboard:**
1. A text strip: "A small study hints that a short walk may help some students."; the hedge words "small", "hints", "may", "some" are highlighted.
2. A two-row word board appears: weaker words (some, most, may, suggests) in a calm colour; strong words (all, every, always, proves) in a hot colour.
3. A choice card "A study proves that a walk raises every student's score" lands on the board; "proves" and "every" flash; the card is stamped "too strong".
4. A second card "A small study hints that a walk may help some students" matches the text's words and turns green.

**Labels & formulas:** `weaker`, `strong`, `too strong`, `matches the text`. No formulas.

**Stress:** hedge words in the text and the flashing strong words; green for the matching card. Words wrap on a phone; at least 12 px.
