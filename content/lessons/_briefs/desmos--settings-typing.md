# desmos--settings-typing

**Shows:** three small fixes that stop wrong answers: the fraction/decimal button, the degrees/radians switch, and the right arrow that leaves a denominator.

**Storyboard:**
1. Frame "Fraction or decimal": a result line shows 0.875; a small toggle button beside it is pressed and the result flips to \(\frac{7}{8}\).
2. Frame "Degrees or radians": two calculator lines. First `sin(30)` = -0.988 in a "radians" state (the wrench icon highlighted, the setting labelled "Radians"); then the setting flips to "Degrees" and the same input gives 0.5.
3. Frame "The right arrow": the keystrokes `y=3/x+2` are typed and the picture shows \(y = \frac{3}{x + 2}\) with the +2 inside the denominator highlighted red. Then `y=3/x` is typed, a right-arrow key icon is pressed, `+2` follows, and the picture shows \(y = \frac{3}{x} + 2\) with the +2 outside.
4. Caption strip: "Compare the picture with the problem."

**Labels & formulas:** 0.875, \(\frac{7}{8}\), "Radians", "Degrees", `sin(30)`, -0.988, 0.5, `y=3/x→+2`, \(\frac{3}{x + 2}\), \(\frac{3}{x} + 2\).

**Stress:** the toggle press, the setting flip and the arrow key press are the three actions; each gets the accent colour for one beat. The wrong picture (red) must be visibly different from the right (green). The arrow key icon at least 20 units wide for a phone.
