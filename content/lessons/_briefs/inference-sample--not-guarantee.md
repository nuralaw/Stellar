# inference-sample--not-guarantee

**Shows:** different random samples give different estimates around one fixed true value, so one estimate never proves an exact value.

**Storyboard:**
1. A number line with a hidden true value (shown later as a dashed line). One sample estimate lands at 38% with its band 33% to 43%.
2. A second sample drops in at 41% with its band, then a third at 36%. All three bands overlap the true value.
3. The dashed line is revealed at 39%. A tick shows that none of the estimates is exactly 39%.
4. Three statement cards appear: "exactly 38%" (crossed out), "the next sample will show 38%" (crossed out), "plausibly 33% to 43%" (ticked).

**Labels & formulas:** \(38\% \pm 5\%\); "sample 1", "sample 2", "sample 3", "true value".

**Stress:** the three bands stacked so the eye sees them moving around the same line; ticks and crosses at the end. Short labels for phones.
